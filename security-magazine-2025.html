Security magazine is a bold and introspective platform born from our personal experiences as Easterners navigating life in the West. The name Security serves as a double entendre: it symbolizes both a haven for those seeking safety and understanding, and a poignant nod to the unsettling moments when security has been called against us for merely existing. This magazine is a collective scream—a visceral expression of identity, frustration, and resilience—manifested through visual art, fashion, interviews, and academic discourse.
